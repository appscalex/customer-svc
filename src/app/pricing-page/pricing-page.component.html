Pricing FAQ

Click any question below to explore answers regarding billing, packaging, and commercial terms.

How is pricing structured for AppScaleX?
Pricing is designed around active projects, managed environments, and pipeline execution volume, ensuring early-stage teams and large enterprises only pay for what they use without surprise overages.
Are FinOps and SRE capabilities available separately?
Yes. FinOps cloud cost optimization and SRE incident lifecycle management can be licensed as targeted add-ons to Growth plans or bundled comprehensively within Enterprise agreements.
Can we deploy AppScaleX inside our private cloud or VPC?
Yes. Enterprise customers can deploy AppScaleX runners and control planes directly inside their dedicated AWS, Azure, or GCP Virtual Private Clouds (VPC) or private Kubernetes clusters for full data isolation.
How do we request a custom quote or proof of concept (PoC)?
Simply click 'Book a Demo' or 'Contact Sales' to share your team size and repository requirements. Our engineering solutions architects will schedule an introductory technical consultation and tailor a trial environment.

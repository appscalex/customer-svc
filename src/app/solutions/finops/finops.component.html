FinOps & Cost Optimization FAQ

Click any question below to explore answers regarding cloud cost governance with AppScaleX.

How does AppScaleX estimate cloud costs?
AppScaleX analyzes provisioned cloud infrastructure telemetry, instance types, and storage allocations against standard provider pricing models to calculate hourly and monthly expenditure estimates.
Does rightsizing affect production availability?
All optimization recommendations highlight potential trade-offs regarding performance and availability. Changes require engineer approval and can be tested in non-production environments first.
Can I configure budget alerts per project?
Yes, AppScaleX allows teams to define environment-level and project-level spending thresholds with email and webhook notifications when expenses approach limits.
